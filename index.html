Regulatory output

The reports we focus on.

Submission-ready outputs for the liquidity reports that matter most to regulated institutions, backed by transparent data lineage and built-in validation.

FR 2052a

Complex Institution Liquidity Monitoring

Granular daily 5G reporting of cash flows and collateral positions for large banking organizations.

FR Y-9C

Consolidated Financial Statements

Quarterly consolidated balance sheet, income statement, and supporting schedules for holding companies.

LCR

Liquidity Coverage Ratio

High-quality liquid assets measured against projected net cash outflows over a 30-day stress window.

NSFR

Net Stable Funding Ratio

Structural funding measure comparing available to required stable funding over a one-year horizon.

NCCF

Net Cumulative Cash Flow

Horizon-based survival metric measuring net cumulative cash flows under a combined stress scenario.

ILST

Internal Liquidity Stress Testing

Configurable scenario engine for idiosyncratic, market-wide, and combined liquidity stress.

Intraday

Intraday Liquidity Monitoring

Real-time tracking of intraday positions, throughput, and peak usage against monitoring metrics.

BCAR / CCAR

Capital Adequacy and Stress Testing

OSFI Basel Capital Adequacy Reporting alongside Federal Reserve comprehensive capital analysis.

Data discovery

02

Data collection

Ingest from warehouses, lakes, and core systems on a governed schedule with provenance captured at the point of arrival.

Cleansing and standardization
